# DPU K-Food Customer Report

A data report on DPU K-Food, a student-run Korean food pre-order and pickup business at DePauw University (Greencastle, Indiana), Sep–Dec 2024.

It covers 156 customer visits by 72 people across 8 pickups, plus catering and a group order: who bought, who came back, why growth stalled, and what the team would do differently.

View the report: open `index.html`, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

All customers are anonymized. No names, phone numbers, or raw order data are included in this repository.
